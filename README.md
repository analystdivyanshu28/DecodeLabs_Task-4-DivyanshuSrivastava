# 📊  Week 4 Task – Data Visualization of E-Com sales analysis.

## 🚀 Project Overview
This Power BI dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of e-commerce sales performance through 
interactive visualizations and KPIs. The dashboard helps businesses track revenue, monitor product performance, 
analyze customer purchasing behavior, and measure operational efficiency.
_______________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________
## 🎯 Key Objectives

* Monitor overall sales performance.
* Identify top-performing products.
* Analyze customer payment preferences.
* Evaluate referral source effectiveness.
* Track order fulfillment and delivery rates.
* Support data-driven business decisions.
_______________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________
## 📌 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

💰 **Total Revenue Generated**
🛒 **Total Orders**
📦 **Total Quantity Sold**
🚚 **Delivery Rate (%)**

________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________
## 📈 Dashboard Visualizations

### 💰 Revenue Trend Analysis

* Line & Column Chart
* Displays yearly revenue performance.
* Helps identify growth trends and seasonal patterns.

### 💳 Revenue by Payment Method

* Donut Chart
* Shows revenue contribution from various payment methods.
* Highlights customer payment preferences.

### 🏆 Top Products by Revenue

* Clustered Bar Chart
* Identifies products generating the highest revenue.
* Helps focus on best-selling products.

### 📦 Product Quantity Analysis

* Column Chart
* Displays quantity sold by product.
* Supports inventory and demand planning.

### 🔗 Revenue by Referral Source

* Pie Chart
* Analyzes revenue generated from different referral channels.
* Measures marketing campaign effectiveness.
